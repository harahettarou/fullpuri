Cliopatria-derived historical political territories for 古プリ比較システム

Source and citation:
Bennett et al. (2025), “Cliopatria: a geospatial database of world-wide
political entities from 3400 BCE to 2024 CE”, Scientific Data.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04516-9
https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria

Changes made for this website:
- Records limited to POLITY entities and to years through 1885.
- Japanese labels and aliases added from Wikidata and a reviewed name table.
- Geometry simplified for web display without inventing new boundaries.
- Records divided into chronological buckets for on-demand loading.
- Display color indexes generated from approximate adjacency.
- All pre-1886 boundaries marked “概略・境界不確実” and rendered dashed.

GeaCron and CHGIS geometry is not included in this derivative.
